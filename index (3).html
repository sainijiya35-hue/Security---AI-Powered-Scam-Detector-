About Project

Sentinel AI – Scam & Fraud Detector

What it does. Sentinel AI scans messages and URLs for common scam and phishing indicators and explains each warning sign with a 0–100 risk score.


How it works. A local, rule-based engine with weighted risk scoring: each detected pattern (urgency, OTP requests, fake bank alerts, prize bait, shortened links, brand look-alike domains, etc.) adds points, capped at 100. It is not a trained machine-learning model.


Risk levels. 0–29 Low · 30–59 Medium · 60–79 High · 80–100 Critical.


Privacy. Everything runs in your browser. No servers, no API keys. Scan history stays in localStorage.


Limitations. Results are preliminary. A low score does not prove a message or site is legitimate, and the URL scanner does not check live reputation databases. When unsure, contact the organization through its official channel.


Built with: HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, Font Awesome, Google Fonts.
