# 🛡️ SENTINEL AI – AI-Powered Scam & Fraud Detector

**Stay Ahead of Every Threat.**

SENTINEL AI is a modern cybersecurity web application designed to help users identify online scams, phishing messages, fraudulent URLs, suspicious emails, and social engineering attacks.

It uses an intelligent, rule-based risk assessment engine to analyze suspicious content, calculate risk scores, identify warning signs, and provide actionable safety recommendations.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Online scams, phishing messages, fraudulent links, and impersonation attempts are becoming increasingly sophisticated.

SENTINEL AI aims to provide users with an easy-to-use security assistant that helps them recognize potential threats before they become victims of online fraud.

### 🎯 Main Objectives

- Analyze suspicious SMS messages, emails, and WhatsApp messages.
- Detect potential phishing and social engineering attempts.
- Identify suspicious website URLs.
- Calculate risk scores based on suspicious patterns.
- Explain potential security threats.
- Provide practical safety recommendations.
- Maintain a history of previous scans.

---

## ✨ Features

### 1. 📊 Security Dashboard
- Total messages analyzed.
- Total scams detected.
- Safe messages statistics.
- High-risk threat statistics.
- Overall security overview.
- Recent scan activity.
- Threat analytics and charts.

### 2. 💬 Message & Email Scanner
Analyze suspicious messages to identify:
- Fake bank alerts.
- OTP and password requests.
- Lottery and prize scams.
- Urgent payment requests.
- Fake KYC updates.
- Impersonation attempts.
- Suspicious links.
- Social engineering tactics.

### 3. 🌐 URL Safety Scanner

Analyze website URLs for suspicious characteristics, including:
- HTTP versus HTTPS.
- IP-address-based URLs.
- URL shorteners.
- Suspicious domain extensions.
- Misspelled brand names.
- Unusual URL characters.
- Suspicious subdomains.

**Note:** URL scanning provides a preliminary assessment based on URL characteristics. It does not guarantee that a website is safe or malicious.

### 4. 🚨 Risk Assessment Engine

The application calculates a risk score between 0 and 100.

| Risk Score | Risk Level | Indicator |
|---|---|---|
| 0–29 | Low Risk | 🟢 |
| 30–59 | Medium Risk | 🟡 |
| 60–79 | High Risk | 🟠 |
| 80–100 | Critical Risk | 🔴 |

The results include:
- Risk score.
- Risk classification.
- Suspicious patterns.
- Threat explanations.
- Recommended safety actions.

### 5. 📜 Scan History
- Automatically saves scan results.
- Stores scan history using browser localStorage.
- Displays previous scan results.
- Shows scan dates and risk scores.
- Allows users to clear scan history.

### 6. 🧪 Sample Test Cases

The application includes sample messages for testing:

1. Safe ordinary message.
2. Fake bank account blocking message.
3. Lottery winner scam.
4. Fake KYC update message.
5. Suspicious payment link message.

### 7. 🔐 Security Tips
Provides practical cybersecurity recommendations to help users stay protected against online fraud.

## 💻 How to Use

1. Open the SENTINEL AI application.
2. Navigate to the Message Scanner.
3. Paste a suspicious SMS, email, or WhatsApp message.
4. Click the Analyze button.
5. Review the generated risk score.
6. Check the detected suspicious patterns.
7. Follow the recommended safety actions.

### To Scan a Website

1. Navigate to the URL Scanner.
2. Enter the website URL.
3. Click Analyze.
4. Review the URL risk assessment and warnings.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

SENTINEL AI is designed with privacy in mind.

- Message analysis is performed locally in the browser.
- User messages are not intentionally transmitted to external servers.
- Scan history is stored in browser localStorage.
- Suspicious URLs are not automatically opened.
- No account registration is required.

---

## ⚠️ Limitations & Disclaimer

SENTINEL AI is an educational cybersecurity assistance project.

- Detection is based on predefined rules and weighted risk scoring.
- It is not a trained machine learning model.
- It does not perform live domain reputation checks.
- It cannot guarantee the detection of every scam.
- A low-risk result does not guarantee that a message or website is legitimate.

Users should independently verify suspicious communications through official channels.
